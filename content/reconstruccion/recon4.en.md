---
title: IV. THE TENSION THAT OPENS
section: RECONSTRUCTION
---

## NARRATIVA

The morning started badly. I do not know why. There was no sign, nor noise, nor shadow. But something was twisted. Something was out of place. Something was waiting to break.

The breakfast glass trembled when I picked it up. Not because my hands trembled. Because the glass trembled. As if it did not want to be held. As if it rejected my touch. As if it knew something that I did not know.

I left it on the table with more force than necessary. The blow resonated. Too much. As if the table were hollow. As if the house were hollow. As if I were hollow.

I went out to the street. The air was tense. Not cold. Not hot. Tense. Like a string stretched too far. Like a muscle about to break.

People walked fast. Too fast. As if everyone were in a hurry to reach a place that does not exist. As if everyone knew that something is about to happen. Something that no one wants to name.

On the corner, a man argued with a woman. They did not shout. But every word was a blow. Every sentence was an edge. Every silence was a threat.

I approached. I do not know why. Perhaps because the tension attracted me. Perhaps because I needed to see something break that was not me.

The man struck the wall with an open palm. The sound was dry. Precise. Violent. The woman stepped back one pace. I stepped back two.

The man looked at me. He did not see me. He looked through me. As if I were air. As if I were noise. As if I were nothing.

I left. Fast. Too fast. My heart beat against my chest as if it wanted to get out. As if it wanted to flee. As if it wanted to leave me behind.

I took a deep breath. The air did not enter. The air did not want to enter. The air was tense. Like me.

And in that tension, something opened. Something that did not want to open. Something that should not open. Something that could no longer close.

## ENSAYO

Tension is not rupture. Tension is announcement. An announcement that does not use words, that does not use gestures, that does not use visible signs.

Tension is the way the world has of preparing a change. Not an abrupt change. Not a violent change. An inevitable change.

Tension is not aggression. Tension is structure. A structure that stretches, that adjusts, that settles before transforming.

The glass that trembles does not announce danger. It announces transition. Matter anticipates consciousness. Matter knows before we do. As in general relativity, where matter warps space-time before light can announce it, internal tension warps perception before the mind can name it.

The argument on the corner is not conflict. It is resonance. A resonance between bodies trying to reorganize themselves. Tension is an attempt at order, not an attempt at destruction.

The heart beating against the chest is not fear. It is preparation. The body prepares for a state that has not yet arrived. Tension is a bridge between what is and what will be.

But the bridge does not know if it will withstand the crossing.

## POEMA

Tension opens.  
  
It does not break.  
  
It does not shatter.  
  
It does not destroy.  
  
Tension stretches  
what the world  
can no longer hold.  
  
The glass trembles.  
  
The street breathes.  
  
The voices strike.  
  
The air hardens.  
  
I open  
without wanting to open.  
  
I break  
without wanting to break.  
  
Tension advances.  
  
And I cry  
because I do not know  
if I should follow it  
or stop it.  


## CIERRE

The afternoon arrived without softness. It arrived as things arrive that do not ask permission. As things arrive that were already here.

I walked toward home. The tension advanced with me. It did not follow me. It did not guide me. It passed through me.

The hollow in my chest adjusted to the movement. The tension settled into it. Not to break it. To open it.

Tension opens.  
And I open with it.
