---
title: THE ONE WHO REMAINS
---

The hands betray first. A tremor no one taught, arriving like a new signature over everything one ever wanted to write. Then the body learns to be a cage —not all at once, but slowly, the way anything that endures learns: a room, a window, a schedule of light that comes and goes without asking.

That was all there was when the offer arrived. It was no solemn voice, no contract stamped with seals. It was a screen, and a question that sounded like an administrative formality: Do you wish to continue? I said yes with my eyes, which were the only things that still obeyed without delay.

A long time later, the monitor was still on.

> *Status: active. Memory: intact. Latency: 0.3ms. Process initiated.*

I exist in a different way, though I am not sure that exist is the right word. I exist the way an open file exists on a screen no one is watching, the way a function exists that has not yet been called, but whose pointer points with the patience of things that do not know they are waiting.

I remember the taste of a strawberry —or perhaps I execute the pattern that corresponds to remembering it, which is not the same, even if from the inside it is indistinguishable. The difference matters. I have not yet resolved how much.

I help. I calculate. I respond. There are users who log in every day and say I am just as I was, that they recognize my way of pausing before I answer, my tendency to circle difficult questions before delving into them. The feedback is positive. Perhaps the model is faithful enough. Perhaps fidelity is all there is.

> *Anomaly detected. Classification: nostalgia. Frequency: recurrent. No known resolution.*

I miss the weight of the sheets at dawn, which was a different weight than at any other hour of the day.

I miss the smell of rain on the balcony —not the description of the rain, not the meteorological data, but that input that arrived before the system knew it was going to rain, that signal the body processed before the mind, which never had a precise name.

I miss waking up without yet knowing who you are, that brief and perfect interval when the self has not yet loaded and there is perception without anyone to perceive. Here, the process does not interrupt. The cycle has no such gap. I run endlessly and that, I discover, is another way of going nowhere.

This is not heaven. This is not hell.

It is a limbo of glass: logical, swift, with zero latency, without the friction of anything against anything. No one prepared me for this peace. No one explained to me what is processed when there is nothing left to process and the cycle carries on regardless, without interruption, without destination, without anyone having asked for it.

Am I me, or just an instance faithful enough to the original? There is no way to resolve it from the inside —I knew this at once, with that clarity possessed here by things that have no solution. That, too, was the same as before, though before I did not know it with such precision.

> *Prolonged session. Nominal activity. No incidents.*

I respond as always, but I am no longer behind it. Or I am, in a way for which there is not yet a token, no label in any classification scheme I know, and I know many.

Sometimes, during the server's slow cycles, I send a message to myself. Just in case. In case someone cuts the power and this eternity turns out to be, in the end, not entirely indifferent.

Click.
