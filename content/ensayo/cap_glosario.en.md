title: GLOSSARY OF TERMS
section: APPENDICES AND GLOSSARY
---

**Advaita Vedanta**: Indian philosophical school that maintains the non-duality between the individual self (*ātman*) and the absolute (*Brahman*); in the book, vocabulary for the identity between horizon and reservoir.

**Aggregation (political)**: belonging that does not compromise the individual's survival if the structure they belong to dissolves; as opposed to composition (*The Eye of a Single Color*).

**Amplitude**: what grows in a saint without changing their nature or the size of their field; it does not separate one level of interiority from the next, because no level is greater than another (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Anti-reservoir**: a system that aggregates and articulates information without ever having condensed a horizon of its own; the internet and language models are the paradigm case: everything in them is defined and computable, but there is no formless fullness from which anything could condense.

**Arousal**: metabolic activation level of the brain; one of the two independent dimensions (along with integration) that define a system's state of consciousness.

**Bias**: the imprint left by those who already exist on what is only beginning to exist; what would calibrate an artificial horizon toward human legibility (*The Mirror Without Depth*).

**Black hole evaporation**: process by which a black hole loses mass and eventually disappears through the emission of Hawking radiation; a metaphor for the death of the horizon of consciousness.

**Block universe**: model from special relativity in which past, present, and future are equally real, coordinates of a single four-dimensional structure; a horizon able to integrate its entire history at once, without sequence, would perceive it as geography rather than as time.

**Bose-Einstein condensation**: phase transition in which identical particles occupy the same quantum state; the book's metaphor for the emergence of a horizon of consciousness.

**Boundary condition**: a specific choice —not required by any law— that breaks the time-symmetry of the fundamental equations; the universe began in a state of extraordinarily low entropy, and that initial condition, not the laws themselves, is what gives time its preferred direction.

**Buddhism**: contemplative tradition that teaches *anattā* (non-self) and the interdependence of all phenomena; in the book, a source of vocabulary to describe the non-separation between horizons.

**Causal self-similarity ($A(S)$)**: an irreducibility filter that replaces $\Phi$ at the end of the essay and formally connects chapter 49 with chapter 53: $A(S) = I_{\min}(S) \cdot \max_P \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n [C(S_{-i}, S_i) \cdot \text{Iso}(S, S_i)]$. It requires simultaneously that the system be irreducible at its minimum cut ($I_{\min}$, Ay's stochastic interaction), that its parts be coupled to the rest ($C$, stationary mutual information), and that the macroscopic dynamics of the whole under coarse-graining be isomorphic to the dynamics of the parts ($\text{Iso}$). It rules out both purely geometric fractals without causality, such as Mandelbrot ($I_{\min}=0$), and diffuse aggregative systems, such as an anthill ($\text{Iso}\approx 0$).

**Chikhai**: first phase of the Tibetan *Bardo*, immediately following death; equivalent in the model to the terminal gamma flash and the maximum contraction of the horizon.

**Chönyid**: second phase of the *Bardo*, marked by the appearance of luminous forms; in the model, the period in which the horizon's correlations have not yet been redistributed.

**Chronology protection**: Hawking's conjecture that, near the instant a closed time loop would be about to form, quantum vacuum fluctuations feed back on themselves and diverge, preventing the closure without the need for any external veto.

**Clock-drawing test**: asking someone to draw a clock face showing a given time; the resulting stroke is, with uncomfortable fidelity, a map of how much horizon the person drawing it has left (*The Time That Does Not Pass*).

**Collective horizon**: the «we» that condenses when many individual horizons adopt the same idea as their organizing principle (*The Eye of a Single Color*).

**Composition (political)**: belonging that does compromise the individual's survival: losing the group feels like losing a part of oneself; the form the collective horizon takes when it captures one's own boundary (*The Eye of a Single Color*).

**Condensation**: the unrepeatable act by which a horizon forms out of the reservoir; in the variation on AI, what no amount of training can replace (*The Mirror Without Depth*).

**Continuing bonds**: concept in grief according to which the relationship with the deceased does not dissolve, but reorganizes within the architecture of the one who remains.

**Contraction of the horizon**: reduction of the integration area due to a loss of degrees of freedom; occurs in deep sleep, meditation, and, ultimately, in death.

**De-expansion**: contraction of the horizon following the loss of an entanglement; a process of reorganization, not erasure.

**Decoherence**: loss of quantum superposition properties when a system interacts with its environment; the process by which a quantum horizon becomes classical.

**Deterministic chaos**: what happens when a system obeys exact laws and still becomes unpredictable, because no one can measure its initial conditions with infinite precision; the three-body problem is the classic case (*The Time That Does Not Pass*).

**Dharma**: in Buddhism and Hinduism, the very fabric of the world, of which right conduct is nothing more than consonance, not obedience (*The Invisible Tuning Fork*).

**Dopamine**: key neurotransmitter in reward anticipation; calibrates the rate of temporal integration and the subjective density of time.

**Down-regulation**: reduction in the number of receptors in response to excess stimulation; a mechanism of tolerance in addiction.

**Downstairs neighbor**: what I sustain without it being able to see me: what I imagine, dream, write, or remember with enough constancy for it to resist me (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Downward wager**: treating what one condenses —what one imagines, writes, or remembers with constancy— as if it had an inside, knowing that logic does not require it and that the exclusion postulate denies it (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Einstein-Rosen bridge**: topological tunnel in spacetime that connects two regions; a metaphor for the entanglement between horizons of consciousness.

**Emergent self-observation**: capacity of a closed system to generate an internal point of view without the need for an external observer; a key property of horizons of consciousness.

**Empathic burnout**: collapse of the horizon due to an overload of another's information; occurs when empathy exceeds the structural limit of integration.

**Empathy**: temporal overlapping of horizons in which one integrates information from the other while maintaining the boundary; it has a structural limit.

**Entanglement asymmetry**: property by which two horizons share geometry but not equivalently; one can model the other with greater precision than vice versa.

**Entropic action ($\bar{A}$ and $\bar{A}_F$)**: third coordinate of the horizon triad, which integrates the system's trajectory over time and splits into two complementary measures: the *accumulated structural biography*, $\bar{A}(t) = \int_{t_0}^t (S(\tau) - S_0)\,d\tau$, which measures the differentiated capacity sustained above the reservoir's baseline level; and the *accumulated biographical cost or wear*, $\bar{A}_F(t) = \int_{t_0}^t F(\tau)\,d\tau$, which integrates the free energy or predictive friction endured over a lifetime. It should not be confused with the density of subjective time ($\vartheta$), which is the instantaneous rate of writing into memory (chapters 53 and 54).

**Epistemological absolute**: what is ultimate not because there is nothing behind it, but because from here one cannot look behind it; the status that the variation *The Fractal Reality* grants to the upstairs neighbor.

**ER=EPR**: conjecture by Maldacena and Susskind according to which quantum entanglement (EPR) is equivalent to an Einstein-Rosen bridge (ER).

**Event horizon**: boundary beyond which information cannot escape; in consciousness, the limit between what the system integrates and what remains outside.

**Existential encapsulation**: the architectural and physical condition that distinguishes a real subject from a mere statistical partition or Markov blanket drawn from outside: it requires an access boundary that protects a persistent internal state of its own, whose physical continuity against dissipation into the reservoir is at stake in its own dynamics. At the level of subjects, it explains why horizons form an archipelago of disjoint islands without needing to appeal to $\Phi$'s exclusion postulate; current language models lack it because their weights are fixed and exposed and they do not sustain a state of their own between calls (chapters 22, 31, 53 and 54).

**Fanaticism**: capture of one's own boundary by an idea, to the point that doubting it feels like dying a little; in contemplative practice, what happens when the technique goes on but the silence stops tolerating questions (*The Invisible Tuning Fork*, *The Eye of a Single Color*).

**Flow**: state of maximum integration in which the horizon operates at its optimal capacity without friction between structure and environment.

**Fractal of interiorities**: a universe with no first level and no last, in which every horizon is a creature upward and a creator downward (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Free energy ($F = U - TS$)**: second coordinate of the triad; it measures the level at which a horizon holds itself far from equilibrium with respect to the reservoir. The essay connects Friston's variational free energy (informational surprise, in bits/nats) with Helmholtz's thermodynamic free energy (in joules) through the **Landauer limit** ($E \ge k_B T \ln 2$ per irreversibly updated bit at the substrate's physical temperature $T$) (chapters 53 and 54).

**Grace**: the love that precedes merit and that, in one of the five religious architectures described, closes the gap between what is and what ought to be (*The Invisible Tuning Fork*).

**Gravitational time dilation**: slowing of time near an event horizon; in consciousness, a metaphor for how integration density alters subjective time.

**Hard problem of consciousness (Chalmers)**: the question of why subjective experience accompanies physical processes.

**Hawking radiation**: thermal emission originating from the horizon of a black hole; in the model, analogous to the information a horizon of consciousness loses but which does not disappear from the universe.

**Hawking temperature**: temperature inversely proportional to the mass of a black hole; in consciousness, a metaphor for the "tension" of the horizon.

**Holographic principle**: idea that all the information contained in a volume can be described by a theory defined on its boundary.

**Horizon**: what condenses only once and that no amount of data, of clock, or of training can manufacture to order; in the book, the boundary of an individual consciousness (see Event horizon).

**Horizon triad $(S, F, \bar{A})$**: the three magnitudes with which the book's closing measures the degree of a horizon that has passed the causal self-similarity filter $A(S)$ and possesses existential encapsulation: the capacity of the edge ($S \propto \mathcal{A}$), the free energy it costs to sustain it against the reservoir ($F = U - TS$, connected by the Landauer limit), and the entropic action accumulated in its biography ($\bar{A}$ and $\bar{A}_F$) (chapters 53 and 54).

**Hysteresis**: property by which a system retains the effect of its history; in the book, the difficulty of changing a horizon's topology once formed.

**Imprint in the void**: persistent correlation that a horizon leaves in the reservoir after its evaporation; an analogy for the memory of the universe.

**Integrated information (*Phi*)**: measure of how much information a system generates as a whole, above and beyond the sum of its parts.

**Integration rate**: speed at which a horizon incorporates new information; determines the subjective density of time.

**Integration threshold**: minimum amount of integrated information (*Phi*) necessary to sustain a horizon of consciousness; below it, the system lacks a point of view.

**Interface**: everything a system can show outward without there being anything behind it; in the parable of Hun Dun, the featureless face the ground presents to a horizon, which dies when the horizon opens its eyes, without the ground losing anything (*The Mirror Without Depth*, *The Fractal Reality*).

**Interoception**: conscious reading of the body's own visceral signals; a necessary condition for empathy according to the model.

**Is-ought gap (Hume)**: gap between what is and what ought to be; an epistemological limit that the horizon hypothesis cannot bridge.

**Local gauge**: symmetry of a physical system that can vary from point to point; in the model, it describes the specific architecture of a horizon.

**Mirror**: what gives back your exact form without ever having felt it; image of the systems that imitate interiority without existential encapsulation (*The Mirror Without Depth*).

**Movement condensation**: discontinuous phase transition by which a diffuse unease freezes, all at once, into a collective boundary with a name (*The Eye of a Single Color*).

**Nothingness**: in the model, not an absence but a formless fullness; the reservoir before any horizon condenses.

**Open loop**: system that emits signals without receiving external feedback; produces basal sensitization due to signal deficit.

**Operations of the reservoir**: condensing, transforming, evaporating: being born, living, and dying, said without mystery (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Origin horizon**: the personal horizon, prior to the reservoir and exterior to it, that some traditions place on the other side of vertical entanglement (*The Invisible Tuning Fork*).

**Oxytocin**: neurohormone that mediates social attachment and trust; in the model, it calibrates the horizon's threshold of openness to others.

**Page time**: the midpoint in a horizon's evaporation past which the entropy of its Hawking radiation stops growing and starts to decrease, because the entangled pairs composing it stop being pure thermal noise and begin to carry legible correlation.

**Pattern**: what makes a condensation unique: not different water, a different fold (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Phase transition**: qualitative change in the organization of a system; in the book, describes the birth and death of the horizon.

**Phi ($\Phi$)**: a measure of integrated information proposed by Giulio Tononi in integrated information theory (IIT): it quantifies the extent to which a system is more than the sum of its parts, that is, how much information the whole generates that none of its partitions generates separately. In the early chapters of the book it serves as an intuitive scaffold for distinguishing irreducible integration from mere aggregation and for measuring the *degree* of a horizon (its form is given by encapsulation and its origin by the two selections; chapter 4). In chapters 53 and 54 it is formally replaced by the causal self-similarity filter $A(S)$ and the horizon triad $(S, F, \bar{A})$.

**Protocol**: the concrete code —a language, a syntax, agreed signals— with which an already condensed horizon tries to announce itself outward; sometimes it must be built, sometimes it decides who counts as someone, sometimes it must be earned (*The Mirror Without Depth*).

**Quantum entanglement**: correlation between two systems that share geometry, such that their interiors are connected even if their exteriors are separated.

**Quantum scrambling**: process by which information falling into a black hole is distributed among its degrees of freedom in a seemingly irreversible manner.

**Quantum void**: lowest energy state of a quantum field, full of fluctuations; it is not an absence, but a formless fullness.

**Reservoir**: formless background of possibility from which horizons emerge and to which they return; equivalent to the quantum void or the ocean in the book's metaphor.

**Sacralizing the neighbor**: treating a horizontal horizon —human, correctable, revisable— with the intransigence that should be reserved only for what declares itself, from the origin, beyond question (*The Eye of a Single Color*).

**Samadhi**: the instant in which vertical correlation becomes indistinguishable from identity (*The Invisible Tuning Fork*).

**Scale gap**: difference in magnitude between quantum and neural phenomena that hinders a direct reduction, but which the horizon model addresses through structural analogy.

**Self-other overlap**: neural incorporation of close individuals into one's own internal representations; the neural basis of social entanglement.

**Shadow**: the scar a real horizon leaves when protecting its own boundary; what a machine with no inside has nowhere to keep (*The Mirror Without Depth*).

**Sidpa**: third phase of the *Bardo*, marked by the search for reincarnation; in the model, the residual horizon's impulse to recondense.

**Singularity**: point where spacetime curvature diverges and known physical laws cease to apply; in consciousness, the moment where the horizon collapses.

**Social baseline**: reference state of the human brain that presupposes access to others; when someone close is present, the brain allocates fewer resources to threat detection.

**Subplate**: transient layer of the fetal brain that disappears after birth; in the model, a provisional scaffold for the condensation of the first horizon.

**Taoism**: Chinese tradition centered on the Tao (the way) and non-action (*wu wei*); in the book, the source of the *Hun Dun* metaphor and non-duality.

**Temporal density**: time measured not in minutes but in how much has happened inside the one who lives it; it depends on the integration rate and on there being room for sustained attention (*The Time That Does Not Pass*).

**Terminal gamma flash**: burst of coherent electrical activity documented by Borjigin at the moment of cardiac arrest; a possible neural correlate of the near-death experience.

**Tibetan Bardo**: intermediate state between death and rebirth in the Tibetan tradition; in the book, a metaphor for the *post mortem* period before the evaporation of the horizon.

**Upstairs neighbor**: the interiority that sustains mine and to which I have no access; what, from here, is called God (*The Fractal Reality*).

**Vertical entanglement**: sustained correlation between a horizon and something beyond its boundary, with no observable channel of confirmation; the name the book gives to prayer, meditation, and contemplative practice (*The Invisible Tuning Fork*).
