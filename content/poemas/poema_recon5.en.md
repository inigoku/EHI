---
title: RECONSTRUCTION — V: THE RUPTURE THAT INSISTS
section: RECONSTRUCTION (POEMS)
---

The rupture insists.

It does not warn.

It does not wait.

It does not forgive.

The rupture opens
what I wanted
to keep closed.

The square breathes
with a new hollow.

The man advances
with an old absence.

I run
because I do not know
how to hold
what breaks.

The rupture insists.

And I weep
because I do not know
if I should stop it
or let it advance.
