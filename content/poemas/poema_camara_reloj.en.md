---
title: THE TIME THAT DOES NOT PASS — WHAT DOES NOT FIT IN A CLOCK
section: THE LAST BOOKS
---

I can measure the shadow crossing the yard,
the laden ant on its way to the curb,
the exact silence a bird leaves
once it has gone.

None of those units converts into the other.
None fits on the face of a clock.

The clock does not lie: it counts the same
for the boy waiting under the olive tree
and for the man who can no longer wait for anything.

But between the two hands
there is a time that will not be counted,
that thickens or empties without permission,
and that is measured only afterward,
by how much of that afternoon
still weighs in your arms.
