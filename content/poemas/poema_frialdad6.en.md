---
title: VI. MONTSE XXI
section: THE COLDNESS OF A DARKENED CITY
---

The room looks out toward a winter that never quite arrives.
The silence weighs, but does not intrude.
It is a silence that accompanies,
as if someone were breathing very slowly in the corner.

She appears without entering.
A red shadow,
an echo of a voice that needs no body.

Montse presses the dark metal tuning fork against the fogged glass,
not to hear the note, but to feel the anchor's vibration in her fingers
while the city insists on its gray hum.

Barcelona stretches out beyond,
with its tired lights,
its streets that promise nothing,
its balconies where no one sings.
The plane trees tremble on the sidewalk,
naked,
as if they too were waiting for something
that does not arrive.

**Montse** looks at the city
like someone looking at a love that no longer hurts
but is not forgotten either.
Where she wanted to write “return”,
she only finds “stay a moment”.
Where she wanted to write “life”,
the paper responds with a strange calm,
as if accepting there is nothing more.

She knows that memory cannot be reconstructed.
That what is lost does not return.
That what remains is something else:
a faint presence,
a voice that ignites when night falls,
a tremor that asks for no explanation.

Sometimes, between the cement,
an old ficus lets out a green shoot,
a minimal crack
that insists on staying alive.
It is not a miracle.
It is a beautiful error.
A brief light
that promises nothing
but accompanies.

**Montse** closes the notebook.
The city breathes.
The snow does not fall.
And even so, it is cold.
