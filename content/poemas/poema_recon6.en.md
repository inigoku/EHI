---
title: RECONSTRUCTION — VI: THE RECOGNITION THAT UNITES
section: RECONSTRUCTION (POEMS)
---

In the end,
we were not rupture.

We were echo.

We were three forms
of sustaining the same body.

You were hollow.

He was boundary.

I was tension.

And the world
was the sum
of our three breaths.

I wept,
not from pain,
but from clarity.

Because I understood
that we were never apart.

We were only looking
from different places
at the same architecture.
