---
title: III. LAMENT OF THE RETURN
section: THE COLDNESS OF AN EXTINGUISHED CITY
---

**I.**
The pact with the light is over.
The week turns like an old door
and I enter the day
with an exhaustion that cannot be explained.
The bread is heavy.
The water does not arrive.
Thirst seeks a place to stay
and finds none.
I, who once had a space of my own,
return to this place that does not recognize me.
I sew the hours with a fine thread,
as if I could sustain myself this way.
I expect no miracles.
Only that it hurts a little less.

**II.**
Line 1 of the subway smells of hot metal and damp coats.
My shadow got trapped in the automatic closing of the door,
like a thin skin I failed to pull away in time.
The world is an inventory of ironed shirts
and names filed away before being pronounced.
At the office, the vending machine coffee
leaves a plastic residue on the palate.

**III.**
Look at my hands.
They still keep the salt of a body no longer here,
the tremor of something that opened too soon,
the mark of a shackle I did not break.
To the office I bring no tools.
I bring this:
a bone gnawing from within,
a tremor no one hears
but which sustains the entire building.
Light is a debt.
And I pay it however I can.
