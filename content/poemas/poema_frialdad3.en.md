---
title: III. LAMENT OF THE RETURN
section: THE COLDNESS OF A DARKENED CITY
---

**I.**
The pact with the light is over.
The week turns like an old door
and I enter the day
 with an exhaustion that cannot be explained.
I, who once had a space of my own,
return to a place that does not recognize me.
I sew the hours with a fine thread.
I expect no miracles.
 Only that it hurts a little less.

**II.**
Line 1 smells of hot metal and damp coats.
 My shadow got trapped in the closing of the door,
like a thin skin I didn't know how to take with me in time.
 The world is an inventory of ironed shirts
and names filed away before they can be spoken.
 At the office, the vending-machine coffee
leaves a plastic residue on the palate.

**III.**
Look at my hands.
 They still keep the salt of a body no longer here
and the pulse of something that opened too soon.
 To the office I bring no tools.
I bring this:
 a tremor no one hears
and that holds up the entire building.
 Light is a debt.
And I pay it however I can.
