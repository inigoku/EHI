---
title: RECONSTRUCTION — III: THE WEIGHT OF LIGHT
section: RECONSTRUCTION (POEMS)
---

Light is heavy.

It does not illuminate.

It leans.

It rests.

It breathes.

Light seeks hollows.

It finds cracks.

It inhabits concavities.

It stays
where the world
withdraws.

Light remembers
what matter
cannot say.

Light holds
what time
lets fall.

Light is heavy.

And I am heavy with it.
