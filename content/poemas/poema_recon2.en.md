---
title: RECONSTRUCTION — II: THE FORM THAT RETURNS
section: RECONSTRUCTION (POEMS)
---

My mother appeared
on the corner.

She was not there.

But she appeared.

The way it appears,
the form
that never left.

The light held her
for an instant.

The air remembered her.

The street
opened a hollow
so she could be there.

She did not speak.

She did not look.

She did not breathe.

But she was there.

The way it remains,
the mark of the glass.

The way it remains,
the crack in the crystal.

The way it remains,
the retreat of the water.

The way it remains,
the minimal concavity
of the soap.

My mother appeared
because matter
remembers.

Even when it seems
that it remembers nothing.

The form returned.

And I returned with her.
