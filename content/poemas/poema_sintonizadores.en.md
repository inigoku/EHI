---
title: VII. THE KNOT OF THE MIX
section: THE COLDNESS OF A DARKENED CITY
illustrationTitle: The Knot of the Mix
illustrationDescription: A horizon line that fragments and dissolves into a metallic grid, reflecting the collapse of the Absolute Tuner.
---

The soul, a recipe book closed in its enclosure,
an ancient basement, a door rusted and tense;
keeps among its remains a defenseless light,
a hidden chest, without cipher or writing.

The Faustian conjuring of an unparalleled seer,
of the closed orb, its rigid prison;
of the soul it is its snare, fatal premonition:
the path already twists, without power to return.

But if the window of confinement cracks,
between the old bolt and a primordial glow,
the false mortar reveals its secret truth.

The body, now ashes of a mineral origin,
its prison of foundations no longer holds it:
it crosses the dark veil, free the immaterial gift.
