---
title: RECONSTRUCTION — IV: THE TENSION THAT OPENS
section: RECONSTRUCTION (POEMS)
---

The tension opens.

It does not break.

It does not shatter.

It does not destroy.

The tension stretches
what the world
can no longer sustain.

The glass trembles.

The street breathes.

The voices strike.

The air hardens.

I open
without wanting to open.

I shatter
without wanting to shatter.

The tension advances.

And I weep
because I do not know
whether to follow it
or stop it.
