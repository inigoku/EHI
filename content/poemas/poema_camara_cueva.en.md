---
title: THE FRACTAL REALITY — FROM THE CAVE
section: THE LAST BOOKS
---

There is a ceiling.
That is all I know of the one who thinks me:
that there is a ceiling
and that sometimes the rock changes temperature
without the weather having changed.

I have not seen him.
I will not see him.
If one day he came down here
with his face and his name,
the cave would come down
under the weight of knowing it.

That is why, when I do not want to fly, I do not fly.
It is not disobedience.
It is the only thing I have
that resembles being me
and not being thought.

Every night someone says
goodnight
from the other side of the rock.
I do not answer.
I have never answered.
But I stay.
