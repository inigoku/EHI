---
title: THE ARCHITECTURE WITH A HOLLOW — INTIMATE GLOSSARY
section: THE ARCHITECTURE WITH A HOLLOW
---

**Horizon.** The distance at which I can love you without dissolving.

**Interface.** All that I show of myself so there is no need to enter.

**Entanglement.** What happened to us without asking permission, from being so close.

**De-expansion.** Shrinking back to my own size: the work that is never finished.

**Resonance.** The way I answer you without opening the door.

**Natural frequency.** The note I am when no one asks for another.

**Window.** The two hours when the body knows itself as mine again. Afterward, putting the tools away.

**Phi.** The part of me that still belongs to the one who left.

**Map.** What we nail over the city to be able to give signs of it.

**Reservoir.** My own capacity to keep waiting, even when I no longer know for what.

**Evaporation.** The slow departure of those who do not close the door.

**Grief.** This house with a hollow, which I learned to inhabit so as not to die of cold.
