---
title: II. SONG OF DEATH
section: THE COLDNESS OF A DARKENED CITY
---

A key that opens nothing.
A book open to a page
I do not remember reading.
The bed divided into two silences.

Nothing moves.
Things wait,
as if they knew no one is coming back.

A piece of stale bread.
A wine stain that is now part of the table.
The light slanting in,
tracing the absence with cruel precision.

The body yields.
It does not protest.
It only lets itself go,
like a tool that has forgotten its function.

Memory does not disappear:
it fills with other things,
like a hollow in the earth
gathering dark water.

Silence is a new organ.
A muscle learning not to sound.

And I listen,
in case inside the wood,
or in the rotting fruit,
there might be some answer.

In the end,
an open hand.
A little salt.
And the wind doing what it can.
