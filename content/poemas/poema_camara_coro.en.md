---
title: THE EYE OF A SINGLE COLOR — CHOIR
section: THE LAST BOOKS
---

A voice alone trembles.
A thousand voices together do not.

That is why one seeks the choir:
not to sing better,
but to stop sounding alone.

Inside the choir no one asks
whether the note they sing is their own
or the one lent to them at the door.

Outside the choir, afterward,
every voice trembles again
as if something had stayed inside.

There are choirs that come apart
as soon as the song ends.
And choirs that no longer let anyone out,
that mistake singing together
for never again being able to sing alone.

The difference is not in the song.
It is in whether, once the choir is over,
you still remember your own voice
without needing a thousand more
to tell you which one it was.
