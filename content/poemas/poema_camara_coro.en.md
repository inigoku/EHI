A voice alone trembles.
A thousand voices together do not.

That is why one seeks the choir:
to stop trembling alone,
even though the note stays the same.

Inside, no one asks
whether the note they sing is their own
or the one lent to them at the door.

At the edge of the plaza,
a single voice keeps trembling,
and still remembers which one it was.
