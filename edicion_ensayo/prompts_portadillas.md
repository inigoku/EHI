# Prompts de las portadillas de sección

Cada sección del ensayo abre con una página impar: lámina + nombre de la sección.
Los capítulos conservan sus propias láminas; las portadillas son imágenes nuevas, una por sección, que no repiten ninguna del libro.

**Formato de salida:** cuadrado 1:1 (la lámina se compone a unas 4 × 4 pulgadas), mínimo 2048 × 2048 px, sin texto.
**Dónde guardarlas:** `edicion_ensayo/imagenes/portadilla_<id>.jpg`, con el id del primer capítulo de la sección (la misma imagen vale para es/ca/en y para los tomos):

| Sección | Fichero |
|---|---|
| Primera parte — El ciclo del horizonte | `portadilla_cap1.jpg` |
| Segunda parte — El horizonte en vínculo | `portadilla_cap7.jpg` |
| Tercera parte — El horizonte que se rompe y el sujeto | `portadilla_cap13_5.jpg` |
| Cuarta parte — La forma y su prueba | `portadilla_cap22_idempotencia.jpg` |
| Quinta parte — El horizonte más allá de la persona | `portadilla_cap17_real.jpg` |
| Sexta parte — Horizontes de otra escala | `portadilla_cap_religiones_comparadas.jpg` |
| Lecturas topológicas (solo volumen único) | `portadilla_cap17_6_real.jpg` |

| Octava parte — El límite del experimento | `portadilla_cap19_real.jpg` |

Si el fichero no existe, la portadilla sale solo con el nombre.

## Estilo común (pegar al principio de cada prompt)

```
Square book-plate illustration, 1:1. Oil painting on canvas with thick visible
brushwork and palette-knife impasto; painterly, quiet, slightly dreamlike;
fine-art realism, not digital illustration, not cartoon, not anime, not 3D
render, not photograph. Palette of the series: deep teal, petrol blue and
ink-green darks, cold silver-white highlights, a single small accent of warm
amber. One clear central motif, generous calm negative space, a low horizon
line somewhere in the composition. Nothing important within 8% of the edges.
No text, no lettering, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no paper border.
```

## 1 · Primera parte — El ciclo del horizonte

```
Scene: a perfectly round, dark sphere of still water floating at the horizon
of a calm sea at dusk, seen from the shore. Something small rises out of the
sphere's surface as a single curl of silver light (birth); at the other side a
second curl sinks back into it (return). Between the two, a slow circular
trail of faint light closes on itself like a ring. The sea beyond is flat and
silent.
```

## 2 · Segunda parte — El horizonte en vínculo

```
Scene: two glass spheres of different sizes resting a short distance apart on
the surface of a dark sea, each holding its own small horizon inside. Between
them, a thread of amber light crosses the water and is reflected in both; the
water between the spheres is slightly disturbed in concentric rings, as if
each had been struck by the same note. Calm sky, a very thin band of light
at the real horizon.
```

## 3 · Tercera parte — El horizonte que se rompe y el sujeto

```
Scene: a large glass sphere on the shore, cracked across, its horizon line
inside broken into slightly offset fragments. Through the cracks a dark
ocean from below shows, deeper and older than the sphere's own water. On the
wet sand beside it, the shadow of a small human figure standing very still
and watching without touching, a lantern lit at his feet. Cold light, grave
and tender, never violent.
```

## 4 · Cuarta parte — La forma y su prueba

```
Scene: a precise, ordered image — a calm sea at the exact hour when sky and
water have the same tone, with a perfectly geometric ring of light on the
surface repeating itself in smaller and smaller concentric copies toward the
centre (the same form at every scale, applied again and it does not change).
Beside it, a tidy row of four small measuring instruments in brass resting on
a dark wooden pier, one per experiment. Sober, almost scientific, still deeply
painterly.
```

## 5 · Quinta parte — El horizonte más allá de la persona

```
Scene: a quiet shoreline at first light with the sphere of water now
widening beyond a single figure: a dog lying asleep on the sand, a worn
wooden chair, an old stone doorway and a lit window above the shore, each one
touched by a faint amber thread that runs back to a distant sphere on the
sea. The mood is of belonging: places, animals and things hold part of the
horizon. Warm accents slightly more present than in the rest of the series.
```

## 6 · Sexta parte — Horizontes de otra escala

```
Scene: at the horizon, a vast alien ocean under two pale moons and a sky of
slow bands of aurora; in the water, partly submerged, enormous structures that
are neither city nor creature, lit from within. In the foreground, small and
human, the same shoreline of the series with a single lantern, looking out.
Scale is the subject: our small round horizon facing a very different,
immensely larger one, without fear.
```

## 7 · Lecturas topológicas (solo volumen único)

```
Scene: a stormy teal sea on which a single band of water twists into a
Möbius strip that rises out of the waves and crosses itself once, its
two faces catching different light (one silver, one amber). Under the strip
float tiny details of the world seen through it: a tear in rain, a horse's
silhouette, a unicorn made of light, a tree line. Painterly, dramatic but
balanced; the strip is the only sharp-edged form.
```

## 8 · Séptima parte — Dos hipótesis del autor

```
Scene: the same shoreline repeated inside itself: in the sea, a sphere that
contains a smaller coastline with a smaller sphere, and so on, five levels
deep, each reflecting the one above (a fractal of horizons). On the nearest
level, a hull of dark clay, half boat and half earth mound, aground at the
water's edge, with one small lit window. Quiet, strange, patient.
```

## 9 · Octava parte — El límite del experimento

```
Scene: a calm shore at nightfall where the water has just returned over the
mud flats; the line where water meets sand is unmistakable, marked by a thin
band of foam and a row of small lanterns left by people who have gone home.
A single set of footprints leads down into the water and disappears; the
water holds the same stars as the sky. Open, unresolved, quiet; the limit of
the picture is the water's edge.
```
