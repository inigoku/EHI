## Estilo común (pegar al principio de cada prompt)

> Illustration for a literary essay about consciousness, water and the event horizon. Polished, painterly digital illustration, soft lighting, deep teal / navy blues with warm gold accents, clean composition with generous empty space around the subject. Same visual family as a series of covers painted in teal oil.

## Prompts

### 1 · il01 — Burbuja, océano, red
Three emblematic spheres arranged in a triangle, joined by thin luminous arrows that form a continuous cycle: top left an iridescent soap bubble with tiny swirls inside; top right a deep-blue ocean wave curled inside a circle, with gold edge lines; bottom a sphere made of a geometric network of glowing nodes and lines. Pale white background with a faint pastel watercolor halo. NO TEXT, NO LABELS, NO LETTERS anywhere.

### 2 · il_prologo — Nacimiento, vida, muerte
Three tall vertical panels with ornate thin frames, side by side, showing one river in three stages: left, a spring-green valley with a young narrow stream and wildflowers; centre, a wide calm river in a lush valley with a fish visible under the clear water; right, a dried-up cracked riverbed with only a thin wisp of white mist rising from the cracks. Cream parchment background. NO TEXT, NO CAPTIONS, NO LETTERS under or inside the panels.

### 3 · il04 — El espectro gradual de la conciencia
Clean infographic-style illustration, without any writing. Upper row: on the left a broken, cracked digital thermostat with a blank grey screen and two empty buttons; on the right a warm glowing analogue dial with a continuous gradient halo. Lower row: five light bulbs in a line, increasing in brightness from left to right (cold dark bulb, dim bulb, moderately lit bulb, warm bulb, radiant bulb), each with a small symbolic icon drawn beneath it, in order: a thermostat, a tiny worm, a bee, a dog, a human silhouette. A thin arrow runs left to right along the row. Light cream background, dark navy line art, amber glow. NO TEXT, NO NUMBERS, NO LETTERS, NO LABELS of any kind.

### 4 · il06 — Los cinco rasgos
Five horizontal bands stacked vertically, separated by thin navy lines on a pale graph-paper background, each with a purely pictorial scene and no writing: band 1, a thermometer and an on/off switch drawn as abstract shapes; band 2, a neat network of connected nodes next to a tangle of colored cables; band 3, a glowing wooden treasure chest with a padlock, light spilling out; band 4, a small mouse running next to a large elephant walking slowly; band 5, a projector casting a holographic rose onto a disc. Bold flat shapes, navy, amber and teal. In place of numbers, use five small plain dots on the left of each band. NO TEXT, NO NUMBERS, NO LETTERS.

### 5 · il07 — Cómic de Hun Dun
A 3 × 3 comic-page layout, hand-drawn and warm, cream paper. The character is a smooth, faceless, round beige creature (Hun Dun) and two small grey boxy robots with thin arms. Panel by panel: (1) the creature smiles between the two robots; (2) the robots carve small holes in its face with tiny tools; (3) the robots work again, a new hole each day; (4) holes for the eyes and ears; (5) holes for the mouth and nose; (6) the creature looks almost finished and worried; (7) it looks sad and old, face wrinkled; (8) the creature has grown old and grumpy; (9) the creature dissolves into a swirl of stardust while the robots watch. Speech bubbles may exist but must be EMPTY (no words, no symbols, no scribbles). NO TEXT, NO CAPTIONS, NO LETTERS.

### 6 · il11 — El tiempo del vínculo
Two ornate antique hourglasses side by side at the top: the left one with ordinary pale sand, the right one with luminous golden sand. Below them, three framed scenes with decorative borders, in a row: (a) a boy playing with a sandcastle on a sunny beach, slow, warm light; (b) a man in a grey office, working at a computer, a wall clock above him, cold desaturated palette; (c) a couple at a café table in deep conversation, golden suspended light and tiny floating motes. Cream parchment background. NO TEXT, NO CAPTIONS, NO LABELS, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS (the wall clock has no numerals).

### 7 · il16 — Papel, cuero, ancla
Three standing full-body human figures side by side, anatomical style, on a white background. Left: a translucent white figure through which wavy signal lines pass freely. Centre: a dark leather-like opaque figure from which the signal lines bounce off. Right: a pearly translucent figure in which the signal lines enter, swirl in a golden spiral at the chest and re-emerge transformed. Thin blue arrows for the signals. NO TEXT, NO LABELS, NO NUMBERS, NO LETTERS.

### 8 · il_cinco_mapas — Los cuatro mapas
Dark cosmic background with a glowing black hole with accretion disk in the centre. Around it, four hands with pens, coming in from the edges, each drawing on its own aged parchment scroll, arranged around the center: (1) a scroll with a cross over a desert landscape; (2) a scroll with a crescent moon and a star over night sky; (3) a scroll with a lotus flower and an eight-spoked dharma wheel; (4) a scroll with an ocean wave and a sun. At the bottom centre, a fifth blank sheet of paper on which one hand has written a short physics equation in tiny dark ink: replace it by an abstract wavy line, not a real equation. Warm gold and deep blue palette. NO TEXT, NO CAPTIONS, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS anywhere, including the scrolls.

### 9 · il_mapayterritorio — El mapa no es el territorio
An aged parchment map lying on a worn wooden desk with a pair of brass compasses and a few cartographer's tools. The map shows a fantastical coastline, islands, mountain ranges, a river and small village icons, drawn in sepia ink, with all place names replaced by blank little banner shapes (empty ribbons). Warm window light from the upper left, shallow depth of field. NO TEXT, NO PLACE NAMES, NO LETTERS, NO CALLIGRAPHY, NO SCRIPT anywhere on the map or the desk.

### 10 · il_orilla — La orilla de Tarel
At the end of a long wooden pier of a harbour town on stilts, at night, an elderly woman with a leather satchel stands with her hand lowered toward the water. The tide has gone out: the mud flats shine like liquid gold, and from the gold water rise soft glowing outlines of little houses, small figures and faint ghostly shapes, like memories surfacing. Old wooden boats and rusty chains lie on the mud, mooring posts, a warm lantern, deep teal sky. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO WORDS written in the water or anywhere else.

## Frase de refuerzo (añadir al final si el modelo escribe letras)

> The image must contain absolutely no text: no letters, no words, no numbers, no captions, no labels, no signs, no calligraphy, no pseudo-writing, no watermark. Any surface that would normally carry writing is left blank.

## Qué hacer con los resultados

1. Guardar cada resultado con el nombre del fichero actual y el sufijo `_sin_texto`
   (por ejemplo `ilustracion_01_sin_texto.png`) en `src/assets/images/`.
   Así la edición en castellano conserva sus originales con texto.
2. Pasármelos. Yo apunto las ediciones inglesa y catalana (`toc_*_en.json`,
   `toc_*_ca.json`) a los ficheros nuevos y regenero PDF y EPUB.
3. En los tres infografías (3, 4 y 6) los rótulos desaparecen por completo. Si
   al leerlas hacen falta, puedo componerlos yo encima, en la tipografía del
   libro y en el idioma de cada edición, que sale más limpio que dejar que la
   IA escriba letras.
